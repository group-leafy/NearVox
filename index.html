Stop screaming into your friend's ear at loud venues! NearVox is designed specifically for effortlessly talking to friends right next to you in extremely noisy environments — completely offline and off-grid.

Ever been at a loud bar, music festival, or concert where communication is impossible without shouting until you lose your voice? NearVox combines local peer-to-peer audio streaming with enhanced noise suppression to make voice chat clear, natural, and strain-free.

The Ultimate Quiet-Talk Setup

  1. Put on your AirPods (or noise-canceling headphones) and turn on Active Noise Cancellation (ANC).
  2. Enable iOS Voice Isolation and NearVox's Enhanced Noise Reduction.
  3. Talk to your nearby friends at a normal volume — no more shouting or strained voices!

Key Features

  • Enhanced Noise Suppression: Specially engineered audio filtering designed to isolate your voice and eliminate heavy background bass, screaming crowds, and bar noise.
  • Designed for AirPods & ANC: Pairs seamlessly with noise-canceling earbuds to block out ambient venue noise while streaming high-clarity voice directly into your ears.
  • Zero Internet & Cell Signal Required: Works completely offline via local P2P Wi-Fi and Apple Wireless Direct Link (AWDL). Perfect for crowded places with overloaded cell towers.
  • Ultra-Low Latency Streaming: Powered by 16kHz PCM local audio streaming for instant, natural conversations with zero server delay.
  • 100% Private & Serverless: Direct device-to-device connection. No user accounts, no middleman, and zero data tracking.

Perfect For

  • Concerts & Music Festivals: Talk normally during live shows without yelling over heavy bass and screaming crowds.
  • Noisy Bars & Nightclubs: Have quiet, effortless conversations across the table without throat fatigue.
  • Crowded Restaurants & Parties: Easily chat in loud indoor environments with heavy background noise.
  • Off-Grid & Cell Dead Zones: Stay connected locally anywhere with zero internet connection.

Download NearVox today, pop in your AirPods, and experience quiet, effortless conversation in the loudest places!
